Projects

Folders you can pick for a conversation. The default folder is used when none is chosen.

Connections

MCP servers give Atenea tools: Gmail, Calendar, GitHub, Notion… JSON object: name → {command, args, env} or {url}.

Atenea Telegram

Talk to Atenea from your phone. Create a bot with @BotFather and get your user id from @userinfobot.

Atenea voz

Tap to dictate: the words appear as you speak, and the full text stays in the box for you to review and send.

Atenea documentos

Atenea searches these folders, the files you upload, the documents she creates and all your past conversations (by meaning and by words). The index updates every 15 minutes.

Backups and trash

A copy of your chats, memory, settings, artifacts and images is made every night at 03:17 on the second disk (the last 30 are kept). Deleted chats stay in the trash for 30 days.

Trash

Notifications

Push notifications on this phone or computer when Atenea finishes something while you are not looking, needs your OK, asks you a question, or a monitor or scheduled task fires.

Memory

What Atenea remembers about you in every conversation (web and Telegram). Tell her “remember that…” in any chat, or add it here.

Reminders & scheduled tasks

They now have their own space: Tasks, at the top of the sidebar, next to Chat and Code.

Background processes

Long-running commands Atenea started (dev servers, watchers, long scripts).

Permissions

Tools that always ask before running (for example send_email, delete_event). Commands that change files outside the project already ask.
